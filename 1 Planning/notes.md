# Base

- making it waterproof while closed is important -- protects it during shipping, rain, etc.
- making it waterproof while open will be very challenging and probably unnecessary. just needs to resist incidental splashes/light rain
- sealed lead acid or other?
- main panel with keyboard etc lifts up on hinge, secured closed with screws or quarter turn latches, reveals battery and pcbs
- secure battery with industrial Velcro
- secure electronics with ???
- power in should be DC, don't involve wall power at all for safety. find most common DC barrel jack size and use that, maybe provide multiple? USB PD would be nice but seems less reliable/ubiquitous. if you went to use the charger on a job and found that it was lost/damaged, what replacement could you find within an hour?
